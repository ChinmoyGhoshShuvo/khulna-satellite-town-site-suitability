**Title:** Satellite Town Site Suitability, Khulna (KDA Planning Area)

**Summary:** In a 7-member studio team, we screened 10 candidate sites near Khulna and ranked 5 of them with a weighted multi-factor analysis of 18 sub-factors, including accessibility, flood level, salinity and services. Site 9, beside the Khulna City Bypass, ranked first (6.54 out of 10). We then produced a detailed site analysis and a 167-acre land budget for about 58,450 residents.
